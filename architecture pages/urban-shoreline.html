URBAN SHORELINE

FREELANCE: KOL/MAC ARCHITECTURE + DESIGN | 2021

1 / 5

In collaboration with ARUP and NVIDIA, this project was for The National Science Foundation Innovation and Technology Ecosystems design competition, which addressed the national infrastructure need for the replacement of failing shoreline protection in urban areas. Our team researched the history of the urban shore, as well as the problem of the fractal and how it relates to the coastline design to propose a barrier reef system off the western coast of Manhattan. This system would allow for prosperity and growth of all ecosystems that it would be inhabited by. The initial design process consisted of writing scripts that gave way to the natural forms of fractals. The project involved researching the site and history of the issue, which gave way to a secondary design phase that consisted of creating diagrams, drawings, programming scripts for 3D models and renders, and putting together a presentation for the competition.
