THE MACHINE

M.ARCH: MEDIUMS I | 2017

1 / 5

This project consists of a hybridized threshold that borrows architectural and geometrical characteristics from the built environment in New York City. A series of photographs were combined to create a new language. The new threshold speaks to the emphasis on verticality and hones in on the interaction of vertical elements and curvature found within arches and architectural ornament. The final drawing portrays how varying combinations of New York City’s architectural landscape can give way to a technological, machine like language.
