DISRUPTING THE GRID

Project Subtitle or Location

A collaboration with peer Allison Barker for this Capstone Project to create an incinerator and recreational program with future environmental challenges in mind. The incinerator and industrial programs are represented by a rectilinear grid that opposes the organic curvature of the natatorium and recreational programs. We studied how these contrasting programs could be represented in a physical form, and honed in on the moments of the grid intersections that caused a push and pull reaction. The grid became a structural element in which the program suspended from, freeing the ground and allowing for future use in a flood zone. Allison and I worked together in programming, site connectivity, and materiality of the project. We collaborated on creating realistic renders and construction details, as well as effectively presenting our design to critics. Allison focused on the overall form of the project, as well as the context of the project within the site, while I worked on the technical aspects consisting of working with grasshopper scripts and how diagrammatic forms and set parameters could lead to design.
