BIOLUME

M.ARCH: DESIGN V | 2019

1 / 5

This community greenhouse double functions as a power plant that creates and stores energy. The electronic plants create energy inside the hexagonal figures and power the bioluminescent forest above. The energy circuit is designed into the roots through the whole system where community members can harvest the vegetation and energy. Group research was conducted to assess possible future scenarios of human civilization. After carefully considering present pressures and environments of today's current world, categories of research that were honed in on for this project include topics such as technological experimentation, and mind and body. This project aims to embody a more sustainable, more prosperous life and environment for individuals and ecosystems in the future. Different levels of energy flow (high, medium, low) combined with different experimental plant species produce different levels of energy flow, which is noted by color and size in this graphic. There are different prototypes that experiment with different habitats and conditions combined with the level of energy flow and plant type with the goal to produce as many different micro climates as possible for higher levels of experimentation with a vast array of species.
