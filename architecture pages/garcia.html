GARCIA KITCHEN

FREELANCE | 2025

1 / 5

This project consists of interior renderings and technical drawings that were created for a client’s townhouse kitchen renovation in Sunnyside, Queens. A material wishlist was provided by the client, in which these renderings are based off of, and were created to help the client envision a future space that allowed for a more open floor plan and flow between the kitchen, living area and outdoor space. Multiple rendering revisions were performed for this project and this was the final version that was executed to construction completion.
