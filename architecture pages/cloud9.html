CLOUD 9

M.ARCH: DESIGN III | 2018

1 / 5

Cloud 9 redefines boundaries. This high rise residential project aims to blend into the existing aesthetic context on the exterior while presenting a sharp contrast in its interior context by creating an inner world. The sloping roof reflects the sky, bringing it down to a crucial moment at the interception of the ground where the building begins with landscape elements and works its way up with a central exterior atrium consisting of glass walls and balconies. The building acts as a border around the site but also remains open, airy, and has optimal views of Downtown Brooklyn.
