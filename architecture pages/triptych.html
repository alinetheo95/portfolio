ARCHITECTURAL GLITCH

M.ARCH: MEDIUMS II | 2018

1 / 5

A speculative vision of New York City transformed through digital collapse—where iconic architecture fragments and reassembles into new hybrid forms. The Flatiron Building serves as the compositional anchor, traced and systematically distorted through computational workflows, then mirrored and recombined to generate an uncanny trinity of structures. Neon-lit voxelated masses colonize the original facades like digital parasites, rendered in Maxwell Engine with luminous cyan and magenta geometries that evoke the atmospheric dystopia of Blade Runner. The title references both the visual aesthetic—pixelated blocks that appear as rendering errors or corrupted data—and the conceptual framework: architecture caught in a moment of systemic failure, where the built environment glitches between its historical form and an unpredictable digital future. The triptych format reinforces themes of repetition, mutation, and architectural memory in an era where physical and digital realities have collapsed into one another.
