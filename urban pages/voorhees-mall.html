VOORHEES MALL

UNDERGRAD: LANDSCAPE ARCHITECTURE | 2014

1 / 5

A series of landscape architectural studies documenting Voorhees Mall on Rutgers University's College Avenue campus, developed from on-site field drawings for Landscape Design I. The watercolor site plan maps the existing condition—a linear campus green flanked by academic buildings, with a diagonal pedestrian circulation path. Charcoal perspective studies capture experiential views through the mature tree canopy toward campus buildings, exploring depth, framing, and the atmospheric qualities of the landscape. Sectional drawings examine the relationship between buildings, tree forms, and ground plane at multiple scales, while figure-ground diagrams analyze the spatial structure by inverting solid and void to reveal how built mass organizes the landscape corridor.
