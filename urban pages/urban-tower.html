URBAN TOWER

UNDERGRAD: URBAN PLANNING + DESIGN | 2017

1 / 5

Conceptual sketch studies for a vertical mixed-use tower developed in an urban planning and design studio. The drawings explore a stacked programmatic strategy with offset floor plates that create exterior terraces at multiple levels. Plans show a central circulation core with compact residential or office units arranged around it, while elevations reveal a stepping massing that responds to setback requirements and provides opportunities for planted terraces and rooftop vegetation. The axonometric views emphasize the structural logic, including structural diagonal bracing at the base, and the integration of green space within the vertical composition, suggesting an early exploration of biophilic design principles in high-density urban contexts.
