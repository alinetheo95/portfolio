FALLINGWATER

UNDERGRAD: LANDSCAPE ARCHITECTURE | 2014

1 / 5

A gestural charcoal sketch of Frank Lloyd Wright's Fallingwater, exploring the dynamic relationship between cantilevered architecture and surrounding forest landscape. Created as a drawing exercise for Landscape Design I, this study captures the building's iconic horizontal planes emerging from dense vegetation above the waterfall. The energetic mark-making emphasizes the interplay between built form and natural context—solid masses of rock and foliage contrasted against the light-struck concrete terraces suspended in space.
