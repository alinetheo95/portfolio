MONTREAL

FREELANCE | 2018

1 / 5

A commissioned elevation study for a residential project in Montreal, combining line drawing with watercolor-style rendering to explore the relationship between existing architecture and mature landscape. The layered watercolor technique distinguishes between the deep greens of established tree canopies, mid-tone understory plantings, and sunlit lawn, suggesting how vegetation mediates views and softens the built form within the residential context.
