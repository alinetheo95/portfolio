URBAN GATEWAY

UNDERGRAD: URBAN PLANNING + DESIGN | 2017

1 / 5

This studio project is a proposal for a mixed-use development along Route 139 in Jersey City. It will include high-rises and mid-rises along Route 139 with varying heights to capitalize on views of Manhattan. There will be development of integrated green spaces as well as a new light-rail connection that will utilize an old rail line infrastructure that is currently not in use. The idea of the Urban Gateway is to create a memorable landmark upon entering Jersey City in order to make it distinguishable and live up to its potential of being located across the Hudson from Manhattan. This site proposal will focus on improving walking conditions by making intersections more pedestrian friendly and using design features such as a vibrant corridor, place-making, and improved accessibility.
